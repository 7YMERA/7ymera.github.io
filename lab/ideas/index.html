Four motion ideas from the rail component

The clothes rail didn't land, but its parts might. Each playground below keeps one mechanic and drops the clothing: spring physics, turn-to-front, flip, and swinging tags. Try each one, then tell me which belong on the portfolio.

1 · Notes shelf2 · Skill flip cards3 · Journey strip4 · Hang-tags

1 · Notes shelf

Books stand spine-out. Hover or tap one and it swings to face you while its neighbours slide aside. Arrow keys work. On a phone it becomes a swipeable carousel.

Could replace: the Notes folders on the home page

2 · Skill flip cards

Cards tilt toward your pointer. Click one and it lifts into a big view you can spin by dragging, or flip with the buttons. The back says where the skill came from.

Could replace: the skill tree leaves, or become a project card view

3 · Journey strip

Drag a card and the others get shoved and spring back. Drag the empty space to scroll the whole strip with momentum. Flick it and let go.

Could replace: the Journey timeline on a wide screen

Drag sideways. Cards bump into each other.

4 · Hang-tags

Each status pill hangs from a pin. Move your pointer across a tag, or tap it, and it swings and settles. Moving near it is enough.

Could replace: the status pills on the Work cards
