IT Asset Disposal

SLB internship, 2025. Turned an e-waste process run on email chains and Excel into one app.

Deployed internally

Before: items logged one by one in a spreadsheet, then approved through a chain of emails to the IT Manager and Finance Controller.

  • Two-level approval in the app: IT Manager, then Finance Controller, with rejected cases sent back to edit and resubmit
  • Approvers set by location: pick a base and the right approvers fill in, so nobody emails the wrong person
  • Automatic emails at every step through Power Automate, plus a watchlist so colleagues can work on a case together
  • Built for scale: 3NF data model across 10 SharePoint lists, and local collections to get past Power Apps delegation limits
