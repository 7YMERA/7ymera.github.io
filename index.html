TaskMatch 2.0

Skill-based task matching for teams · started as my Final Year Project at UTP (FYP I A-)

Active · rebuilding
FYP2.0

Drag to compare. Same data, before and after the redesign.

Problem

Work goes to whoever is free rather than whoever has the skills, and the same few people end up overloaded.

What it does

Ranks people by skill fit and workload, caps work in progress, and pairs strong performers with learners. The team lead keeps the final say and sees the reason for every pick.

What 2.0 fixed

Login on every endpoint, row-level security on all nine tables, rate limiting, a pytest suite in GitHub Actions, and a full redesign with working light and dark modes.
